# Overnover

## Design principles

- **Simplicity first.** Prefer the simplest design that does the job. Fewer screens, fewer controls, less text.
- **No guessing.** Users should never have to guess how something works or what will happen when they act. If a feature needs explaining, simplify it first.
- **Clear and logical.** Every screen should make sense at a glance: what it shows, what you can do, and what happens next. Avoid anything confusing, ambiguous or redundant.
